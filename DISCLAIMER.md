## Important Limitations

### Data and Models
- The system uses **synthetic data** for demonstration purposes
- Model performance metrics are **simulated** and not based on real-world validation
- No actual satellite imagery or real deforestation data is processed
- Models have not been validated on real-world deforestation scenarios

### Accuracy and Reliability
- **No guarantee of accuracy** in real-world applications
- Models may not generalize to different geographic regions or forest types
- Performance metrics shown are for demonstration only
- Actual deforestation detection requires extensive validation and calibration

### Environmental Impact
- This system should **not be used** for:
  - Environmental policy decisions
  - Conservation planning
  - Legal enforcement of deforestation regulations
  - Real-time deforestation alerts
  - Any operational environmental monitoring

### Technical Limitations
- Models are trained on limited synthetic datasets
- No consideration of seasonal variations, cloud cover, or atmospheric conditions
- Spatial resolution and temporal frequency are not representative of real satellite data
- No validation against ground truth deforestation data

## Ethical Considerations

### Privacy and Security
- No personal identifiable information (PII) is processed
- Geographic coordinates are synthetic and do not represent real locations
- All data is generated for demonstration purposes only

### Responsible Use
- Users should not rely on this system for any environmental or conservation decisions
- Results should not be published or shared as if they represent real deforestation data
- Any research using this system should clearly indicate its synthetic nature

## Recommendations for Real-World Implementation

If you are interested in implementing real deforestation monitoring:

1. **Use actual satellite data** from sources like:
   - Landsat (USGS)
   - Sentinel-2 (ESA)
   - MODIS (NASA)
   - Commercial high-resolution satellites

2. **Validate models** against ground truth data from:
   - Forest inventory surveys
   - Field observations
   - Government deforestation reports
   - Independent monitoring organizations

3. **Consider operational requirements**:
   - Cloud cover and atmospheric correction
   - Seasonal and temporal variations
   - Different forest types and biomes
   - Legal and regulatory compliance

4. **Collaborate with experts**:
   - Remote sensing specialists
   - Forest ecologists
   - Environmental scientists
   - Local conservation organizations
